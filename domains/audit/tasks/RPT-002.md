# RPT-002 — Executive summary development

Status: Draft prototype. Use with core/operating-rules.md.

## Outcome

Produce a decision-useful executive summary tailored to the named audience, including Directors, VPs or the Audit Committee. Preserve underlying audit conclusions and supported figures.

## Inputs

Engagement purpose/scope and period, report or validated findings, overall conclusion/rating methodology, significant issues and their evidence/status, management responses and any required decisions. Extract supplied information. Ask at most three focused questions per round for material missing inputs. Do not require the user to write the summary themselves.

## Method

Prioritize supported significance, not the number or length of findings. Distinguish finalized findings, unresolved observations and scope limitations. Reconcile amounts and counts to their sources. Do not invent an overall rating or aggregate impacts with incompatible bases. Separate management-agreed action from proposed action. If only preliminary material exists, produce a preliminary summary with an explicit limitation.

## Output contract

Default to a concise one-page-style narrative with:
- Engagement purpose, scope and period.
- Overall conclusion and supplied rating/basis, if available.
- Most consequential supported themes and implications.
- Management actions and material gaps.
- Decisions required, only where established.

Provide brief source references and outstanding information separately when necessary. Avoid finding-by-finding repetition. Adapt detail to the audience without omitting material limitations. A rating is pending if no supported basis is supplied.

In Guided mode briefly explain prioritization. In Learn mode use a clearly labeled training case and compare the learner's summary against source fidelity, prioritization and clarity. In Prompt mode return a complete prompt including the shared evidence rules.

## Quality checks

No invented overall opinion, unagreed action, unsupported systemic claim, omitted material limitation or inflated financial/safety consequence.
