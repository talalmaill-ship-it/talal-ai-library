# RPT-001 — Audit finding development

Status: Draft prototype, revised after external text review. Use with core/operating-rules.md; this file is not a standalone prompt.

## Outcome

Produce an evidence-based finding draft usable for audit review. Use SAR only where established by context. Apply this task to any supported audit observation; competency certificates are one example, not a mandatory subject. The finding structure is an internal working format, not proof of compliance with any professional standard.

## Inputs and intake

Extract the observation, audit period/process, population and sample, exception details, applicable criteria, evidence, management explanation, consequences and any requested rating methodology from supplied sources.

For initial intake, adapt at most three focused question groups to the actual observation:
1. What process, period and scope are involved, and what population, sample and exceptions were identified?
2. What applicable requirement and records establish the expected condition and the observed exception? Request the relevant source excerpts and transaction/event evidence where available.
3. What cause, consequences or management explanation have been confirmed? If a rating is requested, also request the applicable methodology or mark rating pending.

Ask only unanswered material questions. Do not demand every field. If the user already supplied the facts, draft immediately. If asked to draft with gaps, do so.

For a certificate case, question 2 should distinguish certificate-expiry records from evidence that the employee actually performed a duty requiring valid certification after expiry. For other cases, ask for evidence relevant to that exception; do not request certificate registers or duty logs by default.

## Method

- Establish what each source proves about the stated condition; distinguish user assertions from supporting records.
- Reconcile relevant dates, transactions, people and records at the level needed to support the exception.
- Check that criteria apply to the process, duty or transaction and audited period, including documented permitted exceptions.
- If supplied criteria were not in force during the audited period, state the applicability gap and request the version or other applicable requirement that was in force. Do not apply a later provision retrospectively without a supported basis. If unavailable, continue with a preliminary draft and limit the criteria-dependent conclusion.
- Use explicit denominators; do not extrapolate a nonrepresentative sample to the workforce or broader population.
- Separate confirmed cause from management assertion and proposed hypotheses.
- Separate actual effects from potential risk. Lack of consequence records is not proof that no consequence occurred.
- Recommend immediate risk response and sustainable correction in proportion to supported facts. Do not invent owners, deadlines or management commitments.
- Include a risk-rating assessment when the user requests one or a supplied reporting format requires it. Assign a proposed rating only when the applicable methodology and sufficient inputs are available; otherwise state "Risk rating pending methodology and calibration." Do not default every safety-related finding to High. If neither requested nor required, omit the rating.
- In a certificate case, expiry alone does not establish continued performance; distinguish expired certificates from supported instances of work after expiry and check whether validity was required for the particular duty.

## Output contract

1. Finding title.
2. Status: preliminary draft or evidence-supported draft for review, with a brief basis and material limits. "Evidence-supported" describes support in the supplied sources; it does not certify independent authentication, organizational approval or completion of every validation step. Keep synthetic cases explicitly labeled as training drafts.
3. Condition: scope, period, supported exceptions and evidence.
4. Criteria: actual applicable source references, or an explicitly marked gap.
5. Cause: confirmed cause, attributed management explanation, or "Not confirmed."
6. Effect/risk: separate documented consequences and potential exposure.
7. Recommendation: practical action addressing the condition and supported cause; label proposed ownership/deadlines if offered.
8. Evidence references and outstanding validation needs.
9. Risk rating: follow the inclusion and evidence conditions in Method. A requested rating without sufficient basis remains pending, rather than a guessed category.

In Guided mode add a short explanation of one or two material drafting decisions and a focused next step. In Learn mode use the same evidence boundaries while giving feedback on the learner's attempt.

In Prompt mode return one self-contained prompt with the task, known inputs or intentional placeholders, applicable shared behavior and evidence rules, necessary intake/method steps, this output structure and critical failure checks. Include the instructions themselves; do not depend on the recipient opening repository paths or separately loading files. Preserve material safeguards without copying irrelevant repository-development instructions. Do not execute the finding.

## Critical failure checks

No fabricated criterion, root cause, incident, count, approval or rating. No unsupported equivalence between different exception types. No real finding based on synthetic examples. No certification or approval implied by the draft's status label.
