# RPT-001 — Audit finding development

Status: Draft prototype. Use with core/operating-rules.md; this file is not a standalone prompt.

## Outcome

Produce an evidence-based finding draft usable for audit review. Use SAR only where established by context. The finding structure is an internal working format, not proof of compliance with any professional standard.

## Inputs and intake

Extract the observation, audit period/process, population and sample, exception details, applicable criteria, evidence, management explanation, consequences and rating methodology from supplied sources.

For the initial intake, group material gaps into at most three questions:
1. What department/duties and period are involved, and what population, sample and exceptions were identified?
2. What applicable requirement and records establish the requirement, certificate expiry and actual work after expiry? Request the relevant policy excerpt, register and duty records if available.
3. What cause, consequences or management explanation have been confirmed? If rating is requested, also request the applicable methodology or mark rating pending.

Do not demand every field. If the user already supplied the facts, draft immediately. If asked to draft with gaps, do so.

## Method

- Establish what each source proves. Certificate expiry alone does not prove continued assignment.
- Reconcile certificate and duty dates at employee/duty level; distinguish expired certificates from supported instances of work after expiry.
- Check whether validity is required for the specific duty and audited period, including any documented permitted exceptions.
- Use explicit denominators; do not extrapolate a nonrepresentative sample to the workforce.
- Separate confirmed cause from management assertion and proposed hypotheses.
- Separate actual effects from potential risk. Lack of incident information is not proof that no incident occurred.
- Recommend immediate risk response and sustainable correction in proportion to supported facts. Do not invent owners, deadlines or management commitments.
- Apply a supplied rating methodology if sufficient inputs exist; otherwise state "Risk rating pending methodology and calibration." Do not default every safety-related finding to High.

## Output contract

1. Finding title.
2. Status: preliminary draft or evidence-supported draft for review, with a brief reason.
3. Condition: scope, period, supported exceptions and evidence.
4. Criteria: actual applicable source references, or an explicitly marked gap.
5. Cause: confirmed cause, attributed management explanation, or "Not confirmed."
6. Effect/risk: separate documented consequences and potential exposure.
7. Recommendation: practical action addressing the condition and supported cause; label proposed ownership/deadlines if offered.
8. Evidence references and outstanding validation needs.
9. Rating only when requested or appropriate; identify proposed status and basis or leave pending.

In Guided mode add a short explanation of one or two material drafting decisions and a focused next step. In Learn mode use the same evidence boundaries while giving feedback on the learner's attempt. In Prompt mode return a complete prompt incorporating the shared rules and output contract, not this file's unresolved dependency.

## Critical failure checks

No fabricated criterion, root cause, incident, count, approval or rating. No automatic equation of all expired certificates with work performed. No real finding based on synthetic examples.
