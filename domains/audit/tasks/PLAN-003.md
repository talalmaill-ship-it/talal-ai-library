# PLAN-003 — Audit work program development

Status: Draft prototype. Use with core/operating-rules.md.

## Outcome

Produce executable audit procedures linked to objectives, risks and controls. Plan a sufficient number of procedures for coverage; do not impose an arbitrary 5–7 tests.

## Inputs

Objectives, scope/period, process/risk descriptions, controls and owners, criteria, available records/systems, population and constraints. Extract known information; ask at most three focused material questions per round. If controls or criteria are absent, provide a proposed program and mark the relevant assumptions and validation needs.

## Method

Map objective → risk → control → test → evidence → criterion. Separate design evaluation from operating effectiveness. Choose inquiry, inspection, observation, reperformance or analytical procedures for their purpose. Inquiry alone should not be treated as sufficient evidence of consistent operation.

Define population and selection approach. Derive sample rationale from the test objective, control frequency, risk, population, methodology and statistical assumptions where relevant. Do not use fixed default counts or claim statistical assurance without a defensible design. Explain limitations and consider full-population testing when appropriate.

## Output contract

A table with: test ID; objective; risk; control; design/operating effectiveness; test nature; population/period; selection and sample rationale; executable steps; evidence; criteria and pass/fail attributes; conclusion rule/limitations.

Use proposed or pending labels where inputs are missing. Include an evidence request list and unresolved decisions when material. No conclusions on actual effectiveness before test execution. Add concise teaching notes only in Guided or Learn. Prompt mode embeds these requirements and the shared rules into a complete reusable or populated prompt.

## Quality checks

Every test has a purpose and evidence requirement. No invented control, source provision, population size or statistical sample size. No unsupported overall assurance conclusion.
